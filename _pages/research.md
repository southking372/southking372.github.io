## Physiological Sensing / 生理感知

**English.** My current Ph.D. research focuses on **physiological sensing**, especially continuous, non-invasive, and reliable sensing in real-world settings. I am interested in combining wearable sensors, physiological signals, and intelligent models to support robust and personalized health monitoring.

**中文。** 我的博士阶段研究主要聚焦于**生理感知**，重点关注真实场景下连续、无创和可靠的健康状态感知。我希望将可穿戴传感器、生理信号与智能模型相结合，构建面向个体化健康监测的稳健感知与分析方法。

## Research Interests / 研究方向

- **Wearable & Physiological Sensing / 可穿戴与生理感知**
- **Physiological Signal Processing and Machine Learning / 生理信号处理与机器学习**
- **Biomedical Artificial Intelligence / 生物医学人工智能**

## Research Foundations / 研究基础

### PPG and Cuffless Blood Pressure Monitoring / PPG 与无袖带血压监测

My previous work investigated non-invasive blood pressure estimation from photoplethysmography (PPG), including self-supervised learning, diffusion models, differential physiological features, and transfer-learning-based approaches. This work provides an important foundation for my broader interest in physiological sensing and continuous health monitoring.

前期研究围绕基于光电容积脉搏波（PPG）的无创血压估计展开，涉及自监督学习、扩散模型、差分生理特征以及迁移学习等方法，为进一步开展生理感知与连续健康监测研究奠定了基础。

### Wearable Sensing / 可穿戴感知

I have also worked on flexible wearable fiber sensors for limb motion recognition and physiological monitoring, which motivated my interest in integrating sensing hardware with data-driven physiological inference.

我也参与了柔性可穿戴纤维传感器相关研究，用于肢体运动识别与生理监测，这进一步推动了我对传感硬件与数据驱动生理状态推断相结合的兴趣。

### Biomedical AI / 生物医学人工智能

My collaborative work in biomedical AI includes breast ultrasound analysis and computational pathology, including efficient deep learning models for lesion detection and segmentation, as well as diffusion models for virtual pathological staining.

在生物医学人工智能方面，我参与了乳腺超声分析和计算病理相关研究，包括用于病灶检测与分割的高效深度学习模型，以及面向虚拟病理染色的扩散模型。
